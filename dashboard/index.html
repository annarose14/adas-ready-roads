ADAS-Ready Roads

Can a lane keeping camera see the painted lane lines on Sydney's roads? An agent screens crowd sourced street photos with OpenCV 5, asks a vision language model on AWS when it needs a second look, checks OpenStreetMap, and sends uncertain places to people.

Research prototype. Verdicts are screening hints to prioritise human inspection, not safety certifications. See "Validated performance" for what has and has not been demonstrated.

Quick tour (about 1 minute)

On 4 new Sydney suburbs, labelled blind, it agreed with people on 72 of 75 decided locations (96%, kappa 0.80) and sent 13 unclear ones to review.

    Validated performance (blind human labels)

    • Latest clean test: 96% agreement (kappa 0.80). 88 new locations, labelled blind: 72 of 75 decided locations correct, 13 sent to review, and no road without lines called "lines visible". Missing markings rest on few examples (27 of 42 found across all tests).
    • First clean test (4 never used suburbs, 56 locations): where painted lines were visible, the system confirmed them in 42 of 48 decided locations (88%, 95% CI 75 to 94%) and sent 7 to human review.
    • OpenCV only versions scored at chance on unseen suburbs; OpenStreetMap alone was a poor guide (54%). Human self agreement on paint wear was poor (kappa 0.14), so wear is not graded.
